---

# 1. Product Overview

CampusNest is a student accommodation discovery platform designed for students relocating to a new city for college.

Instead of forcing students to manually compare scattered accommodation information, CampusNest combines:

* **AI-powered recommendations**
* **Accommodation comparison**
* **Effective-cost calculation**
* **Commute and locality information**
* **Property/owner verification information**
* **Blockchain-backed verification records**

The MVP focuses on one complete journey:

> **Profile → Discover → Compare → Verify → Decide**

The goal is not to build a complete accommodation marketplace during the hackathon.

The goal is to prove that CampusNest can help a student move from:

> **"I need a place to stay."**

to:

> **"These are the properties that fit me, this is why, this is the real cost, and this is what has been verified."**

---

# 2. Problem

Students moving from outside their home state often struggle to find suitable accommodation near their college.

Information is distributed across:

* Brokers
* Social-media groups
* Informal referrals
* Property platforms
* Maps
* Reviews

This makes it difficult to answer simple but important questions:

* Which property fits my budget?
* What will I actually spend every month?
* How far is it from my college?
* How long will the commute take?
* What facilities are available?
* What do residents say about it?
* Has the property/owner been verified?
* Which option is actually the best fit for me?

CampusNest aims to consolidate these decision factors into one experience.

---

# 3. Product Goal

CampusNest should allow a student to:

1. Enter their accommodation requirements.
2. Discover suitable properties.
3. Receive ranked recommendations.
4. Understand why a property matches them.
5. Compare shortlisted properties.
6. See the property's effective monthly cost.
7. See commute and locality information.
8. See available verification information.
9. Open the blockchain verification record.
10. Make a more informed accommodation decision.

---

# 4. Core Product Philosophy

CampusNest has three primary pillars.

## 🤖 AI — "What fits me?"

AI helps interpret the student's requirements and explains why recommended properties match.

## 🔐 Verification — "What has been checked?"

CampusNest displays what information about a property has gone through its verification workflow.

## ⛓️ Blockchain — "Can the verification record be checked?"

A cryptographic record of the verification event is registered on a blockchain testnet so the record becomes tamper-evident and independently checkable.

### Important clarification

Blockchain does **not** independently prove that a property is genuine or safe.

It proves that a particular CampusNest verification record was registered on-chain and has not been silently changed.

# 10. Feature 4 — Match Score

## Purpose

Rank properties according to measurable criteria.

The MVP uses a fixed scoring formula.

| Factor                       | Weight |
| ---------------------------- | -----: |
| Budget compatibility         |    30% |
| Distance / commute           |    25% |
| Trust / verification signals |    20% |
| Facilities                   |    15% |
| Lifestyle preference         |    10% |

The weights may be adjusted after mentor feedback, but the system should use a deterministic scoring method.

## Example

```text
Sunrise PG

Budget:        29/30
Commute:       24/25
Trust:         18/20
Facilities:    13/15
Lifestyle:      9/10
--------------------------------
Match Score:   93/100
```

Displayed to the student as:

> **93% Match**

# 11. Feature 5 — AI Recommendation Explanation

## Purpose

Explain the recommendation in simple language.

AI should answer:

> **"Why is this property a good match for me?"**

Example:

> **"This property is a strong match because it fits your ₹8,000 budget, is only 1.4 km from NIET, and has strong resident feedback for cleanliness and management."**

### Important

The AI does not independently invent the numerical ranking.

The deterministic scoring system produces the ranking.

AI explains the result.

# 15. Feature 8 — Effective Monthly Cost

Rent alone does not represent the student's actual monthly expense.

CampusNest calculates:

> **Effective Cost = Rent + Food + Electricity + Wi-Fi + Maintenance**

## Example

```text
Rent             ₹7,500
Food               ₹500
Electricity        ₹150
Wi-Fi              ₹100
Maintenance        ₹100
--------------------------------
Effective Cost    ₹8,350/month
```

The effective cost should be prominently displayed.

# 19. Blockchain Scope

The MVP uses:

### Network

Polygon Amoy testnet

### Smart Contract

`VerificationRegistry.sol`

### Core functionality

```text
registerVerification(
    propertyId,
    recordHash
)
```

and:

```text
getVerification(
    propertyId
)
```

### On-chain information

* Property ID
* Record hash
* Timestamp

### Never put on-chain

* Student names
* Phone numbers
* Emails
* Identity documents
* Owner personal data
* Sensitive property information

---

# 20. Blockchain Implementation for MVP

To reduce integration risk, blockchain verification will be registered manually before/during the hackathon using Remix.

The resulting real testnet transaction hashes will be attached to the corresponding seeded properties.

The app will display those hashes and link to the blockchain explorer.

### Why?

The MVP's objective is to demonstrate:

> **A real, checkable blockchain-backed verification record**

without making the entire product dependent on a live smart-contract integration during the overnight build.

# 34. Honest Judge Positioning

## If asked: "Does blockchain prove the property is genuine?"

Answer:

> "No. Blockchain does not independently verify a physical property. CampusNest performs a verification workflow first, and blockchain records a tamper-evident proof of that verification event."

## If asked: "Why AI?"

Answer:

> "The problem isn't just finding properties. It's deciding which one fits a particular student. Our scoring system ranks the properties, while AI explains that recommendation in natural language."

## If asked: "Are the reviews real?"

Answer:

> "The prototype uses seeded sample reviews, which are explicitly labeled as demo data. We don't claim they are real user-generated reviews."

## If asked: "Is this production-ready?"

Answer:

> "No. It's a functional hackathon MVP. We've intentionally focused on proving the core discovery, recommendation and verification experience before adding production features such as authentication, payments and full KYC."
